# Imagery

## In brief

This discipline is intentionally undeveloped. Future guidance will cover when imagery is the right communication tool, how it relates to interface hierarchy, and how art direction supports product meaning.

## Open questions

- What job is the image doing that layout, type, or color cannot do alone?
- Is the image explanatory, emotional, atmospheric, evidentiary, or navigational?
- How should cropping, scale, and sequencing respond to the interface around it?
- When should imagery recede, and when should it become the primary content?

Do not infer additional principles until Julian documents them.
