# Crystal Silence

I learned Chick Corea’s “Crystal Silence” on vibraphone while growing up, and the song still means a great deal to me. I use its approximate 76 BPM tempo as personal scaffolding for complex animation.

At 60 frames per second, a 48-frame loop lasts 0.8 seconds and corresponds to 75 BPM. That is close enough to the recording’s tempo to preserve the reference. It gives me a default timing system without requiring me to invent one for every project.

The reference is sentimental and practical. It reduces decision load, keeps related animation synchronized, and gives the motion a consistent starting rhythm.
