# Study design history

I think the best way to learn design is to study history. Interfaces are fundamentally recombinations of graphic design and other forms of design. Historical forms make their underlying decisions easier to see.

Look at manuscripts from before the printing press. Study how information was ordered, repeated, decorated, and made legible before mechanical reproduction standardized the page.

Look at how dobby looms were programmed. Their systems mechanically encoded repeatable patterns long before digital interfaces used grids, tokens, components, and software logic to do related work.

These references provide context for interface design. They are not working rules for agents.
